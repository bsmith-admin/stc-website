What we do

Integrating individual business strategy with working technology to optimize value and business outcomes

To ensure that new or growing companies work in harmony with their organizational systems and available tools, we strive to ensure efficient ERP implementation and provide the foundational knowledge behind the IT strategy it is built upon.

01

Epicor ERP Implementation

We set up and configure Epicor, the system many manufacturers use to run sales, purchasing, production, and inventory in one place, so every department is working from the same information. 20+ full implementations, 50+ manufacturing clients.

02

ERP Consolidation & Standardization

If different locations or departments are each running their own separate systems, we bring them onto one shared platform, so everyone logs in once and sees consistent, accurate data.

03

PLM Strategy & Digital Thread

We connect the tools your engineers design with to the systems that build and ship the product, so a change on the drawing board shows up correctly on the production floor.

04

Fractional IT Leadership

Get the judgment of an experienced IT executive, budgeting, vendor negotiation, long-term planning, without hiring a full-time CIO or Director.

05

Private Equity IT Due Diligence

Before a deal closes, we assess the target company's technology so buyers know what they're actually acquiring: what's solid, what's risky, and what it'll cost to fix.

06

Cloud & Cybersecurity Modernization

We move your systems to modern, secure infrastructure (Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365) and keep testing them regularly, so your business stays protected as threats change.
