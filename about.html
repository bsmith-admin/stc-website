Our story

Two decades of IT consulting, one clear focus

Software Technology Concepts was founded in 2006 to help small and mid-size businesses make sense of their technology, not just install it. Two decades later, that focus hasn't changed, even as the systems have.

In 2026, STC relaunched with a renewed focus on Epicor ERP and modern IT strategy, built for the businesses navigating growth today. We kept what worked and rebuilt the rest.

Our name says it plainly: software, technology, concepts. The tools, the systems, and the thinking that connects them to your business.
