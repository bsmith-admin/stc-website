What We Offer

IT and ERP services built for growing businesses

From full-cycle Epicor implementations to PLM strategy, regulated-industry compliance, and fractional IT leadership, we cover what growing and multi-site manufacturers need to run on modern systems.

01

Business Process Assessment & Workflow Optimization

Mapping how work actually happens today, not just how the org chart says it should, then identifying the bottlenecks, manual workarounds, and disconnects between departments before any system change is scoped.

02

Epicor ERP Implementation & Support

Full-cycle Epicor deployments across Vantage/Vista 6 through Kinetic. 20+ full implementations and delivery for 50+ manufacturing clients spanning Quote-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, Production, and Material Management.

03

ERP Consolidation & Multi-Site Standardization

Replacing or consolidating disparate ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, Syteline, Viewpoint Vista, and others) into one governed platform. A unified general ledger, single sign-on, and standardized configuration across every site.

04

PLM Strategy & Digital Thread Integration

Connecting engineering to execution. PLM strategy integrated with SolidWorks and ERP keeps design data, capacity, and production in sync across the business.

05

Medical Device & Regulated Manufacturing Compliance

Aligning enterprise systems with FDA 21 CFR Part 11, ISO 13485, and EU MDR. E-signatures, audit trails, DHFs, and DMRs built to withstand a real audit, not just pass a checklist.

06

Fractional IT Leadership & Governance

CIO and Director-level engagement for companies that need senior technology leadership without a full-time hire. Budget ownership, vendor and SOW negotiation, and governance standards across multi-site operations.

07

Private Equity IT Due Diligence & M&A Readiness

Pre-acquisition technology assessments for PE sponsors and portfolio companies. Disaster recovery readiness, cyber insurance coverage, technical debt, and integration risk, evaluated before the deal closes.

08

Cloud Infrastructure & Cybersecurity Modernization

Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, and Entra ID/Intune migrations, zero trust rollouts, SOC and RMM deployment, and recurring penetration and spam testing to keep environments modern and defensible.

09

Data, Reporting & Executive Dashboards

SQL Server, SSRS, Crystal Reports, and Power BI dashboards that turn ERP and operational data into the financial position, job costing, and production KPIs leadership actually needs.
