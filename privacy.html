Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information submitted through this website. Last updated September 3, 2026.

Information we collect

When you fill out the contact form on this site, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, company, phone number if provided, the service area you select, and any message you write. We don't collect information any other way, and we don't require you to create an account or log in to use this site.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, schedule a consultation, and follow up about the services you asked about. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

This site relies on a few outside services to work, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Formspree processes and delivers messages submitted through our contact form.
  • Microsoft Bookings (part of Microsoft 365) powers our scheduling calendar, if you book a consultation, that appointment is handled under Microsoft's own privacy terms.
  • Google Maps is embedded on our Contact page to show our general location. Google may set its own cookies when that map loads.

Cookies and analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand overall site traffic and improve the site over time. These tools use cookies or similar technology and collect general, non-identifying usage information rather than anything you personally submit. Embedded third-party content (like the map above) may also set its own cookies, which we don't control.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form submissions as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain a record of the conversation. If you'd like us to delete information you've submitted, email us and we'll take care of it.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, at any time, by emailing info@sfttech.com.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business use and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and revise the date at the top.

This policy is a plain-language summary provided for transparency and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you have questions about how this applies to your specific situation, consult an attorney.
