Why STC

What it's actually like to work here

We're small on purpose. Every consultant works directly with clients and leadership, not through layers of account managers and delivery staff who never touch the actual work.

01

Real client impact

You're on the engagement from kickoff to go-live, not handed a slice of a project you never see the outcome of. Manufacturers who run their business on what you build.

02

Modern tools, not legacy process

Epicor Kinetic, Microsoft 365, and Azure, not a decade-old stack held together with workarounds. We rebuilt STC's own technology from the ground up in 2026, and we practice what we sell.

03

Direct access to leadership

No multi-layer reporting chain. You work with the people who make decisions, not a proxy for them.

04

Ground floor of a relaunch

STC was founded in 2006 and is rebuilding for 2026 and beyond. Join now and help shape what the firm becomes, not just fit into something already set in stone.

05

Focused, not enterprise-only

We work with small and mid-size manufacturers, so the problems are real and the scope stays sane. No 18-month projects that lose the plot halfway through.

06

Flexible by design

Client work is remote-friendly with travel when a project calls for it, not a mandated five-day office routine for its own sake.
